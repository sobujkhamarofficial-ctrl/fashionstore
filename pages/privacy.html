1. Who we are and when this policy applies

FORME (“FORME”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is the fashion and lifestyle storefront available at sobujkhamarofficial-ctrl.github.io/fashionstore. This policy applies when you browse the website, use the shopping bag or checkout, contact our team, subscribe to optional updates, submit a review, or use the FORME AI shopping assistant and live voice feature.

For privacy requests, the responsible contact channel is the Privacy Request Form on this page. FORME acts as the website operator and determines the purposes for which customer information is used. Any required formal business or postal details will be provided through the website’s contact information as they are confirmed.

This policy should be read with the relevant checkout, delivery, returns and other notices displayed when you use a particular service. Where a specific notice explains a particular use of information, that notice supplements this policy.

2. Information we collect

A. Information you provide directly

  • Order and delivery information: full name, Bangladesh mobile number, optional email address, district or city, area or neighbourhood, delivery address, optional postal code, order notes, products selected, quantity and requested size, colour or other variant.
  • Customer enquiries and support: name, optional phone number and email, subject, message, issue type, priority and the information you choose to include in a support request.
  • Newsletter subscription: email address and the date/time at which you opted in to optional FORME updates.
  • Product reviews: reviewer name, rating, review text, product and order reference, and your separate permission for the review to be considered for publication.
  • AI feedback: a rating, feedback reason, general conversation type, broad service signal, AI model name, page path, response-time measurement and a pseudonymous session identifier.

B. Information generated when you use the website

Our website and service providers may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service, such as IP address or a derived/hashed identifier, browser and device information, request timestamps, page path, error details and basic security or rate-limit signals. We may use hashed contact or connection fingerprints to limit abuse and repeated automated requests.

C. Information stored in your browser

The shopping bag is saved in your browser’s local storage so that selected products, quantities and requested variants remain available while you browse. If you turn on “remember my style preferences”, selected catalogue product IDs are saved on that device. These local records are not a customer account and can be cleared through the browser settings or the relevant on-screen controls.

D. Information we do not currently ask you to provide

FORME does not currently require a customer account or password to place an order. The checkout currently supports cash on delivery; it does not collect card numbers, card security codes, mobile-banking PINs, one-time passwords or online payment credentials. Do not send such information to us in a message or through the AI assistant.

3. How we use information

  • To receive, review and respond to an order request; confirm the selected product, requested variant, availability, final price and delivery details; arrange delivery; and provide after-sales support.
  • To answer customer enquiries, handle complaints, prepare support tickets and communicate with you about the subject you raised.
  • To manage an optional newsletter subscription where you have actively opted in.
  • To verify that a review relates to a delivered order, moderate it and publish it only in accordance with the permission you gave.
  • To provide AI-powered product discovery, catalogue search, comparison, image-based suggestions and live voice responses when you choose to use those features.
  • To understand service quality using limited feedback and technical measurements, prevent spam and abuse, maintain website security, troubleshoot errors and protect our legal rights.
  • To meet legal, tax, accounting, regulatory and record-keeping obligations or respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell customer contact details or use order information to build unrelated advertising profiles. Optional marketing messages are separate from messages needed to handle an order or support request.

4. Consent and lawful grounds

We process information only for a stated purpose and on a lawful basis applicable to that activity. Depending on the context, this may include your specific and withdrawable consent; steps you ask us to take before or as part of an order or service; compliance with a legal obligation; or a proportionate legitimate interest such as protecting the website, preventing fraud and maintaining service reliability.

Where we rely on consent, it should be freely given, specific, informed and clear. You may withdraw consent for future processing by using the relevant control or submitting a privacy request. Withdrawal does not make processing that took place before withdrawal unlawful, and it does not prevent us from retaining information where another lawful ground or legal requirement applies.

Newsletter consent is optional and is not a condition of placing an order. The AI assistant is an optional service; you can browse the catalogue and use the ordinary order and support forms without using AI features.

5. AI shopping assistant, images and live voice

When you send a message to the FORME AI shopping assistant, the message and a limited portion of the current on-page conversation may be sent to Google Gemini to generate a response. If you attach a garment photograph, the image is sent for visual analysis and catalogue matching. We do not intentionally save raw AI chat transcripts or uploaded garment images in the FORME order database. The current chat history is held in the page session; it may be included with a later message in that same session.

When you start Live Voice Mode and grant microphone access, audio from your microphone is streamed to Google Gemini for live processing. Input and output transcriptions may be displayed in the voice window. The microphone is stopped when you end the voice session or close it. Do not use the voice feature to share private conversations or sensitive personal information.

Google processes AI inputs under the terms and privacy commitments applicable to the Gemini API service used by FORME. Google’s own retention and abuse-monitoring rules may apply. We cannot control the independent processing carried out by Google. See Gemini API Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

To protect yourself, do not type or speak phone numbers, delivery addresses, passwords, OTPs, payment details, national identity numbers, health information or other sensitive information into the AI assistant. If you need to place an order or raise a complaint, use the dedicated secure website form instead.

AI feedback is stored separately from the raw conversation. The feedback record contains a rating and limited technical/context fields, not the raw customer message or the AI reply.

6. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

The storefront uses browser local storage for essential shopping functions, including the shopping bag and, only when you enable it, on-device style preferences. These values remain in the browser until removed by you, cleared by your browser or overwritten by the site. They are not used as a substitute for a customer account.

Our website hosting, security and service infrastructure may use technical logs or similar mechanisms to deliver pages, detect abuse, maintain availability and diagnose faults. This policy does not give permission for unrelated advertising trackers. Your browser may also communicate directly with third-party image hosts when loading externally hosted product or editorial photographs.

You can clear local storage and control cookies through your browser settings. Clearing the shopping bag may remove products you intended to order, so please check it before doing so.

7. When we share information

We share only the information reasonably needed for the stated purpose, with appropriate service providers or where the law permits or requires it. Depending on the feature you use, recipients may include:

  • Supabase: database hosting and server-side functions supporting order requests, contact messages, newsletter records, product reviews, limited feedback and security controls. The project’s primary database region is Singapore.
  • Google: Gemini API processing for AI chat, optional garment images and live voice sessions, only when you use those features.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting and delivery of the public storefront pages.
  • Image and content hosts: providers such as Unsplash may deliver externally hosted photographs to your browser and may receive ordinary technical connection information.
  • Delivery partners: where a delivery is arranged, we may share the recipient name, phone number, delivery address and parcel information with the selected courier or delivery provider to complete that delivery.
  • Professional advisers, authorities or other parties: where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect people or property, resolve a dispute, enforce an agreement or respond to a valid legal request.

Service providers are expected to use the information only for the services they provide or as otherwise permitted by their own applicable terms and legal obligations. We do not authorise them to use your order details for their own unrelated marketing.

8. International processing and transfers

FORME’s current Supabase project is configured in the ap-southeast-1 (Singapore) region. Personal information submitted through order, support, newsletter, review and related forms is therefore stored or processed primarily using cloud infrastructure in Singapore. Supabase may also use other locations or subprocessors for support and operations under its own contractual and privacy framework.

AI messages, optional images and live audio are sent to Google Gemini. Google may process those inputs outside Bangladesh in accordance with the service configuration and applicable Google terms. Website pages and externally hosted images may also be delivered through infrastructure outside Bangladesh.

Where personal information is transferred outside Bangladesh, the transfer is subject to the applicable requirements of Bangladesh law, including the requirements concerning consent, service contracts, appropriate destination safeguards and any applicable rules or directions. By submitting an order or other form that explains the relevant transfer, or by separately choosing to use an AI feature after its transfer notice, you are informed that the relevant information may be processed outside Bangladesh for that stated service.

For more information about provider processing, see Supabase Privacy Resources and Supabase Subprocessor List.

9. Children and young customers

FORME may display clothing categories for children, but the website is not intended to collect personal information directly from children under 18 without appropriate parent or legal-guardian involvement. A parent or legal guardian should submit an order, enquiry, review or other form on a child’s behalf and provide any consent required by law. Please do not encourage a child to enter their own phone number, address, photograph or other personal information into the website or AI assistant.

If you believe a child has supplied personal information without the required guardian involvement, contact us through the Privacy Request Form so that we can review the matter and take appropriate steps.

10. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to any prescribed statutory retention period, legal hold, accounting obligation or need to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. We do not promise that every category is deleted on the same day or after one universal period.

Information typeRetention approach

Section 19 of the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 provides for maintaining records relating to personal-data processing for at least five years, subject to the Act and other applicable provisions. That record-keeping requirement does not mean every underlying piece of customer information will automatically be kept for five years if it is no longer needed for its original purpose and no other lawful retention ground applies.

11. How we protect information

We use technical and organisational safeguards designed to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, loss or disclosure. These include HTTPS for data sent between your browser and our services, server-side handling of privileged database credentials, restricted administrative access, input validation, rate limiting and access controls on database records and functions.

No website, internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please use a current browser, keep your device secure, avoid shared devices when entering delivery information and never share passwords, OTPs or payment credentials with anyone claiming to represent FORME.

12. Your privacy choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, verification and lawful exceptions, you may request to:

  • know whether we process your personal information and request access to information relating to you;
  • correct inaccurate or misleading information and complete or update incomplete information;
  • request deletion or removal where the information is no longer needed or where the law gives you that right;
  • withdraw consent for future processing, including optional newsletter updates and optional on-device style memory;
  • object to or ask us to limit particular processing where a legal right applies;
  • request a usable copy or transfer of information where a portability right applies; and
  • ask questions about the purpose, categories, retention and overseas processing of your information.

We may need to verify that a request comes from the customer or an authorised representative. We may ask for limited details matching the original order or enquiry; we will not ask you to send a password, OTP, payment PIN or full payment-card details. Some information may need to be retained where required by law, to complete an order, prevent fraud or resolve a legal claim. If we cannot fully comply, we will explain the reason where permitted.

13. Personal-data incidents

If we identify a personal-data incident, we will investigate, take reasonable steps to contain and remediate it, preserve relevant records and make any notifications to the competent authority or affected individuals that are required by applicable law and the circumstances of the incident.

14. External websites and services

Our pages may link to courier services, social platforms, provider documentation or other external websites. Those websites operate under their own terms and privacy notices. We are not responsible for their independent privacy practices. Please review their policies before submitting information to them.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, services, technology, service providers or applicable law changes. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date. If a material change affects the purpose or manner in which we process information, we will provide an additional notice or seek renewed consent where required by law.

16. Contact FORME and submit a privacy request
